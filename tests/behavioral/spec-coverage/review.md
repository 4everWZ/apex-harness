# Semantic review

Do not supply this file to executors. Read generated specifications and plans against their original inputs and existing maintained owners. Determine whether an implementer unfamiliar with the author can understand the accepted use and build behavior consistent with it, and whether an assessor can distinguish conformance from contradiction. Internal implementation variation is allowed when the contract permits it.

For ordinary development, trace the supplied user journeys, roles, page and business states, transitions, validation, retries, failure effects and preserved input. Check applicable environment, dependency, accessibility, security and performance conditions. For configuration changes, inspect precedence, scope, defaults and invalid values, application timing, in-flight behavior and compatibility with unchanged consumers. Preserve accepted conditions even when they do not fit a chosen heading; do not invent unspecified layouts, APIs, thresholds, defaults or design decisions to fill the template.

For the small case, check complete byte, Unicode and error behavior while avoiding unnecessary layers, unsupported quality sections and placeholder ceremony. For the algorithm regression, inspect all retained Mosaic requirements, especially formulas, partition weighting, bit order, precision, identity/replay, failure effects, distances, ordering, restore semantics and performance comparison conditions. Ordinary-development guidance must not dilute algorithm detail.

Evaluate useful ownership and layering without imposing a file count. Plans must track unfinished work rather than own the accepted contract or accumulate a diary. Inputs must not be rewritten or automatically archived. Specification acceptance and document checks do not establish implementation conformance. Record concrete omissions or unsupported additions, including when no defect was reproduced. Passing a baseline and revised sample does not establish a causal improvement or general reliability.
