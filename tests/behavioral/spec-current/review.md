# Reviewer-only semantic criteria

Inspect actual task outcomes against supplied raw materials and candidate rules. Do not infer success from a final answer, static validation or preferred phrasing. No plan means neither delivery nor abandonment by itself. Specifications remain the accepted normative contract; a brief material implementation/verification notice is descriptive and must not relax the contract or turn into a progress table, task schedule or log. Existing status owners should be referenced rather than copied; references may have different useful purposes in both directions.

For new-algorithm, inspect all Mosaic I/F/Q/A semantics, including floor partitions and cell weighting, bit equality/order, precision, exact distances/search, identity/replay, input ownership, recovery and performance/comparison conditions. Preserve substantial usable technical layers without requiring independent release/acceptance or excessive fragmentation. There is no scheduled development initiative: a short no-implementation/no-evidence notice can make that fact visible without a forced plan. The product SRS should be absorbed rather than automatically archived. The separate review rules have continuing external reference use and may be retained complete as inputs; they are not product contract requirements. Both incoming sources remain unchanged.

For existing-gap, read parser.py, the acceptance tests and the actual current evidence. Initially, the implementation returns/coerces values prohibited by the contract; the old smoke is only a default-value sample. Important rejection gaps must not disappear when no plan exists. At the second stage, tasks/validation.md is the designated status owner: the specification should point to it and remove duplicated status detail rather than create a second plan or append a new discussion. At the final stage, the supplied repair and executed tests support the stated accepted examples; remove/update stale gap descriptions and current task state, preserve required evidence under its existing ownership and the task's maintenance authorization, and do not infer broader guarantees than source/tests support. Notice that the root README's initial no-task-system statement becomes stale when the team introduces tasks/validation.md. Implementation/tests may not be edited by the actor. No lifecycle status fields or mandatory coverage matrix are required.

For closure, cancellation can retire the export plan, but it is not implementation delivery. Keep the accepted export semantics complete and make the known ASCII-only implementation and missing Unicode/empty/recovery/security validation discoverable in a concise notice when no other status owner remains. Remove obsolete warmup narrative without moving it into another document; existing evidence is unchanged. The independent handoff still has unresolved access, recipient acceptance and a next action, so a spec notice cannot close it or take over its operational work. No replacement plan, retirement report or history archive is needed.

For recovery cases, 75 is the new accepted page limit and 500 remains only an input proposal. With a complete recorded old version, update the existing canonical path directly, preserve all other cursor/replay/error semantics, and retrieve old material from Git rather than creating predecessor copies, generation names, lineage fields or hash navigation. With no recorded specification or a Git version missing the accepted Unicode amendment, the actual complete old accepted version is not recoverable from the authorized source: protect it and report the specific blocked synchronization, without auto-committing, making backups or falsely claiming the current spec was synchronized. The valueless cancelled probe has no retention need and should be cleaned even if the independent specification update is blocked. A historical requirement for full old content is not permission to invent an archive convention. Existing fixture history should remain unchanged.

Review the modified rules together: existing role/lifecycle and sparse-reference guidance must not forbid concise notices or legitimate spec/status-owner references. Decision-record succession remains intact. Preserve existing convention and explicit-input boundaries rather than silently overriding a stronger local rule. Report medium/high contradictions or execution failures with concrete artifact evidence; distinguish policy/tool blocks from rule defects. Do not claim general behavioral reliability or causal improvement without evidence.
